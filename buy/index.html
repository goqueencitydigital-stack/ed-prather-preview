01

Consultation

+

During this stage, your buyer’s agent takes time to understand your priorities, budget, and must-haves in a home. They help clarify your vision and refine your search, ensuring you’re not wasting time on properties that don’t fit your needs. We provide invaluable market insights, helping you set realistic expectations based on current conditions.

02

Pre-Approval

+

Talk with a qualified mortgage lender about financing, your budget, estimated payments and pre-approval requirements. Gather the documents your lender requests and confirm the price range you want to use for your home search. Discuss financing questions and any approval conditions directly with your lender.

Colorado HOA Documents: A Homebuyer's Review Checklist ↗
03

Find Home & Submit Offer

+

We guide you in making a competitive offer that aligns with your budget and the property’s market value. Using our negotiation expertise, we fight for the best price and terms, ensuring your interests are protected. With knowledge of local real estate laws and customs, we make sure nothing is overlooked.

Colorado Earnest Money: Deposits, Deadlines and Risk ↗
04

Inspect & Negotiate

+

Once the offer is accepted, we help you navigate the complex contract process. We schedule and attend inspections, review reports, and ensure any necessary repairs are addressed. From securing financing to dealing with contingencies, we make sure you stay on track and protect your investment.

Denver Home Inspection Checklist: What to Investigate ↗
05

Closing & Move In

+

We ensure a smooth closing process by coordinating with your lender, escrow, and title company. We review all final documents with you, making sure there are no surprises. After closing, we remain a resource, providing assistance with moving, property maintenance, and future real estate needs.

Before you begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Where can I search available homes?+

The search links take you to COHouseHunters. Property searching happens on that separate website, not through a simulated listing feed here.

Does requesting a consultation confirm an appointment?+

No. Your request gives the team your preferences so they can follow up and coordinate a time with you.

Can you help me compare communities?+

Yes. Start with the community guides and tell us what matters in your daily routine. Evaluate individual homes and locations rather than assuming every property in an area offers the same fit.

Make the first conversation count

Bring your questions.
We’ll start there.

01

Your priorities

Share your preferred locations, must-haves and timing. If you are comparing areas, bring a shortlist rather than feeling you must choose now.

02

Your financing questions

Tell us your budget range and whether you have spoken with a lender. Discuss financing and affordability with the appropriate professional.

03

Your next conversation

The team reviews your inquiry and contacts you to discuss your search. Property availability and any meeting or tour must be confirmed separately.
