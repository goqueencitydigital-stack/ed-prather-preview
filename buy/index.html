We work with first-time buyers, move-up buyers, people relocating to Colorado and homeowners buying while they sell.

01

Buyer Consultation and Search Planning

Every search starts with what you need. We discuss your preferred communities, budget, timeline and must-haves so you do not spend time touring homes that do not fit, and we explain current market conditions so expectations are realistic.

  • Preferred communities and commute
  • Budget, timeline and property requirements
  • Questions about the buying process
02

Pre-Approval and Your Price Range

If you are financing, we encourage you to connect with a qualified lender for pre-approval, then help you understand how the financing timeline fits the purchase so you are ready when the right home appears. Plan cash to close with the Denver buyer closing cost guide.

03

Home Search, Showings and Property Evaluation

Search available homes on COHouseHunters (opens the team’s Colorado home search in a new tab), then tour with your buyer’s agent. We arrange showings and help you look beyond the listing photos.

  • Comparable sales: how the asking price compares with recent local activity
  • Condition: age, visible features and when a professional inspection is needed
  • Ownership costs: property taxes, insurance, HOA fees and maintenance
  • Resale considerations and neighborhood trends
04

Offer Preparation and Negotiation

When you find the right home, we review market information and help you prepare an offer that reflects your goals, budget and the property’s circumstances, then negotiate price and terms on your behalf. We explain contract terms, deadlines and contingencies before you sign.

05

Inspection, Appraisal and Contract Deadlines

After your offer is accepted, we help you schedule inspections, review the findings and negotiate needed repairs. We also track appraisal and document deadlines, including HOA documents for condos and townhomes and what happens if the appraisal comes in low.

06

Closing Coordination

We coordinate with your lender, escrow and title company and review the final documents with you so there are no surprises. After closing, we remain a resource for future real estate needs.

07

Our office is in Lakewood, at 274 Union Boulevard, Suite 450. Our Lakewood buyer’s agents help you compare areas such as Belmar and Green Mountain on daily routes, condition and the full monthly cost, then handle showings, offers, negotiation and closing.

Put your research to work

Build a Colorado home-buying plan before making an offer.

A useful buying plan connects three things: what you can comfortably spend, how you want to live and what you still need to verify about a property. Keep your required features separate from preferences you could compromise on. That makes a shortlist more useful than a collection of attractive photographs.

Colorado’s home-buying guide explains that an inspection covers general condition and major components, while additional inspections may be needed. Treat a showing as an opportunity to identify questions, not a substitute for professional evaluation.

Compare the monthly cost and the upfront cash

Ask your lender to distinguish the down payment, closing costs, prepaid expenses and the estimated recurring payment. Add your own allowance for utilities, maintenance and any association obligations. Use written estimates for the specific property before treating a monthly figure as a spending commitment.

Discuss representation before the transaction advances

Ask how the brokerage relationship, scope of service, agreement term and compensation will be documented. Colorado’s written-agreement advisory explains the role of a written agreement in establishing the brokerage relationship. Read the agreement with your broker and obtain legal advice on questions outside the broker’s role.

Keep the offer connected to your research

Bring unresolved insurance, inspection, title, association and financing questions into the offer discussion. Ask which dates and conditions address them in the proposed contract. If an answer is important enough to affect your willingness to buy, put it on the decision list rather than leaving it in a casual text conversation.
