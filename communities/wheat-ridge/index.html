Find your place in Wheat Ridge.

Use outdoor access as one practical lens for a Wheat Ridge search, then test how each home handles everyday transportation and upkeep. The most useful comparison is between actual properties and usable routes, not broad labels.

01

Start with Prospect Park and the Greenbelt

Prospect Park is a regional, diverse-use park on W. 44th Avenue, between Kipling and Youngfield streets. The city describes it as adjacent to the 300-acre Wheat Ridge Greenbelt and connected to the Peaks to Plains Trail, also known as the Clear Creek Trail. These documented landmarks can give you a concrete starting point when exploring the west side of Wheat Ridge and comparing locations. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

Treat the park and trail connection as points to investigate, not as a measure of what every home nearby offers. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park On a visit, note the particular route you would use to reach the park, the setting around a prospective property, and whether those features suit your routine.

02

Match park amenities to your outdoor routine

Prospect Park’s documented amenities include two ballfields, six pickleball courts, a football field, two playgrounds, two fishing piers, restrooms and a 150-person pavilion. Permanent cornhole boards, horseshoe pits, and improved roads and walkways are also part of the renovated park. Use this list to decide what you would actually want to visit, then check the park in person rather than assuming every amenity will matter to your household. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

The city says the renovations were completed in two phases between 2017 and 2022, totaling more than $1.2 million, with funding from Great Outdoors Colorado and the Conservation Trust Fund. It also describes the improvements as creating more opportunities for people of different ages, backgrounds and abilities to enjoy the park. When scouting, check the walkways and facilities that matter to you directly. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

03

Use Wheat Ridge’s planning tools before you compare

Wheat Ridge’s Zoning and Development Code is Chapter 26 of the city’s Code of Laws and sets standards for land use and development. The city directs property owners and prospective buyers to its Find Your Zoning page or interactive property information map to look up zoning and development requirements. Start with the specific address or parcel you are considering, rather than relying on a broad impression of an area. Zoning & Development Code | Wheat Ridge, CO

The city says zoning regulates allowed land uses and development rules such as setbacks and height restrictions, and that Wheat Ridge has residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use and planned-development districts. These categories are a reason to compare individual properties carefully: nearby parcels may have different designations or requirements. Use the city’s property information map and code resources to guide questions about a particular address. Find Your Zoning

04

Look beyond a district label

The city identifies several mixed-use districts, including Mixed Use Neighborhood, Mixed Use Commercial and Mixed Use Lutheran Legacy Campus. It says the Mixed Use code addresses items such as building height, setbacks or build-to rules, design, open space and parking. If a property appears to be in a mixed-use district, consult the applicable code rather than assuming that a general residential or commercial description answers your questions. Find Your Zoning

Some commercial properties are also in overlay zones, according to the city’s zoning information. For properties in Traditional or Contemporary Overlay zones, the page says a build-to requirement applies in place of the front setback requirement; the overlay map is identified in Figure 2.1 of the Architectural and Site Design Manual. Confirm whether a specific parcel is covered before drawing conclusions about its design or development rules. Find Your Zoning

05

Verify the exact parcel and its zoning

The city’s interactive property information map includes a Zoning Districts layer. Wheat Ridge advises turning off other colorful layers, such as City Council districts, so the zoning layer’s colors display accurately. For a home you are considering, search the address or parcel number and review the district information as a starting point for further checks. Find Your Zoning

Wheat Ridge cautions that its zoning map is updated as frequently as possible but may not show a recent rezoning from the past couple of months. For confirmation, the city asks people to email the Zoning Hotline or call 303-234-5931 and provide a street address or parcel number. This is especially useful when a planned project depends on a particular zoning interpretation. Find Your Zoning

06

Check permits when evaluating changes to a home

Some projects that are exempt from local building codes may still require a zoning permit for Planning Division review. Wheat Ridge lists trash or dumpster enclosures, certain painted or window and door signs, and outdoor recreational equipment greater than 120 square feet and more or less permanent in nature among the projects that may require one. If you are considering an addition or site change, check the applicable city requirements before treating it as approved. Zoning & Development Code | Wheat Ridge, CO

Before applying, confirm the current submission steps, review requirements and fee with the city.

07

Scout routes instead of guessing at travel times

Prospect Park connects to the Peaks to Plains Trail, which the city also calls the Clear Creek Trail and identifies as a National Recreation Trail. The park is also adjacent to the Wheat Ridge Greenbelt. If these connections matter to your plans, visit the relevant trail access points and assess the route from the property you are considering. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

For other transportation needs, check the routes and schedules for your destinations and the times you expect to travel. During a scouting visit, try the routes you expect to use and note where you would want more information.

08

Turn a scouting visit into a useful checklist

Plan a visit around a specific property and Prospect Park: locate the address, explore the park’s documented facilities, and walk or otherwise test the route between them. The park’s location on W. 44th Avenue between Kipling and Youngfield streets can help orient that visit. Record what you want to verify about access, nearby destinations and the property itself instead of relying on a single drive-by. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

For a purchase, gather the parcel number, check the city’s zoning map and code, and ask the Zoning Hotline about uncertainties or recent changes. For a sale, prepare the property details and records a buyer may want to review, and confirm the city requirements relevant to any proposed work. For either path, compare locations against your own needs and schedule follow-up research before making decisions. Find Your Zoning Zoning & Development Code | Wheat Ridge, CO

Questions & answers.

Where is Prospect Park?+

The city places Prospect Park on W. 44th Avenue in Wheat Ridge, between Kipling and Youngfield streets. It also describes the park as adjacent to the Wheat Ridge Greenbelt and connected to the Peaks to Plains Trail, also known as the Clear Creek Trail. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

What amenities does Prospect Park have?+

The city lists two ballfields, six pickleball courts, a football field, two playgrounds, two fishing piers, restrooms and a 150-person pavilion. It also lists permanent cornhole boards, horseshoe pits, and improved roads and walkways. Check the facilities in person to see which fit your plans. City of Wheat Ridge: Prospect Park

How can I find a Wheat Ridge property’s zoning?+

Use the city’s interactive property information map and turn on the Zoning Districts layer, or consult the Find Your Zoning page. The city recommends providing a street address or parcel number when asking questions. Because the map may not reflect a recent rezoning, contact the Zoning Hotline to confirm uncertain or time-sensitive information. Find Your Zoning

Does a project that does not need a building permit still need zoning review?+

It may. Wheat Ridge says some projects exempt from local building codes may still require a zoning permit for Planning Division review. Examples include certain enclosures, signs and outdoor recreational equipment greater than 120 square feet and more or less permanent in nature. Check the city’s requirements for the specific project before proceeding. Zoning & Development Code | Wheat Ridge, CO

Who can help verify a zoning designation?+

Wheat Ridge directs zoning questions to its Zoning Hotline at 303-234-5931 or the email listed on its Find Your Zoning page. Include the address or parcel number. The city cautions that the online map may not reflect a rezoning completed in the past couple of months, so ask the city to confirm when accuracy matters. Find Your Zoning
