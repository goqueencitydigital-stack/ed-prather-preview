Find your place in Northglenn.

For a Northglenn search, map your regular destinations alongside usable trail entrances and station access. Then compare property condition and ownership responsibilities with the same care as location, keeping the analysis specific to the actual address.

Photo credit: Jeffrey Beall

Northglenn’s zoning code describes residential, mixed-use, commercial, and industrial districts. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Start a home search with the official zoning map, then confirm how the code applies to a specific parcel. Use a focused visit to assess the setting and access, and gather property and permit questions before making a decision.

01

Start with Northglenn’s official zoning map

Begin with the Official Zoning District Map rather than relying on a general impression of a block. Northglenn’s code incorporates the map into the code and says it may be examined during office hours at the City Clerk’s office. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Before touring, write down the addresses you are considering and check with the City about office hours if you plan to visit.

The code says district boundaries generally follow the centerlines of streets, roads, highways, alleys, and channelized waterways, unless otherwise stated. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts If a boundary is unclear or disputed, the Director determines its location, and an appeal goes to the Board of Adjustment. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Treat the map as a starting point and ask the City about a parcel-specific question.

02

Compare the residential district intentions

Northglenn distinguishes three single-family districts by intended lot character: RS-1 is for large lots, RS-2 for standard-sized lots, and RS-3 for small residential lots in urban and suburban neighborhoods. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts The code describes RS-1 and RS-2 as low-density residential, while RS-3 is intended for medium- to high-density development. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

Use those descriptions to frame questions, not to assume a particular home’s dimensions or future permissions. Compare each property’s actual lot and building details, then ask the City which standards apply. The code explains that district sections include lot and building standards, and that when an illustration conflicts with its standards table, the table governs. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

03

Look beyond single-family labels

RM-1 is intended for mixed-density housing, primarily single-family attached homes with limited small-scale multifamily uses, and may provide a transition between higher- and lower-intensity districts. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts RM-2 allows for varied housing densities, including attached single-family and multifamily uses, and may also include community, educational, and limited supportive uses. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

The MH district is intended for medium-density single-family and manufactured housing, and may also include community and educational uses. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts If one of these districts appears near a home you are considering, compare the district’s stated intent with your priorities. For a specific use, addition, or renovation, ask the City to identify the applicable standards rather than inferring approval from the district name.

04

Understand Northglenn’s mixed-use distinctions

MN, or Mixed-Use Neighborhood, is intended to remain primarily residential while allowing limited community and commercial uses, particularly retail and services for neighborhood convenience. Its intended mixed-use development is walkable and has an active streetscape. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts MC, or Mixed-Use Corridor, also emphasizes walkable, active streets, with lighter-intensity commercial uses and attached housing among its stated intentions. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

MR, or Mixed-Use Regional, is intended for large-scale development or redevelopment serving the City and region, with regional employment and commercial activity and limited higher-density housing. Its stated intent includes automobile access and circulation as well as pedestrian-friendly development and access to public amenities and public transportation. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Compare these purposes, then verify the conditions around each address in person.

05

Check the commercial or industrial context

Northglenn’s CG district is intended for a broad range of commercial uses, including retail, office, services, small-scale business parks, and cultural and community uses. The code says CG uses often include auto-oriented uses, while also promoting pedestrian-friendly development where appropriate. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts These are district purposes, not a description of every current business or a guarantee about a proposed use.

CA is intended for commercial and limited light industrial uses, often with auto-oriented activity and larger parking and loading areas than other commercial and mixed-use areas. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts IN is intended for industrial or heavy commercial activities such as warehousing, storage, distribution, research and development, and limited processing or manufacturing, mostly inside enclosed buildings. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts If nearby uses matter to you, confirm mapped districts and applicable rules.

06

Explore Northglenn’s Art on Parade

Add a cultural stop to a Northglenn scouting visit: Art on Parade is an outdoor sculpture-on-loan program displayed at E.B. Rains Jr. Memorial Park, with sculptures installed around Webster Lake. Northglenn Arts, Art on Parade

The program invites residents and park visitors to vote for a sculpture to be purchased and added to the City’s permanent art collection. Northglenn Arts, Art on Parade E.B. Rains Jr. Memorial Park is across from City Hall at 11701 Community Center Drive. Northglenn Arts, Art on Parade

Check the program’s current exhibit details before visiting. While you are there, consider whether the park setting and public art are a fit for your interests; assess each candidate property’s immediate surroundings separately, and verify routes and services with current providers.

07

Separate district purpose from parcel approval

A district’s purpose is not the complete rulebook for a property. Northglenn’s code says the base-district sections describe purpose and intended character, followed by lot and building standards and district-specific development standards. It also notes that cross-referenced “other standards” do not represent every standard that may apply to a development or application. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

Before an offer or project decision, ask the City which zoning standards apply to the exact parcel and proposed work. Separately request available permit and improvement records, and compare them with the property’s visible condition and seller-provided information. These checks are practical steps, not conclusions about a home’s compliance, future use, or the outcome of an application.

08

Make your Northglenn scouting visit count

Plan a focused visit around the properties you are comparing. If you need to inspect the official map in person, the code identifies the City Clerk’s office as the place where it may be examined during office hours. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts On the property tour, note observed access, street activity, and nearby uses, then check those observations against your priorities rather than assuming they will remain unchanged.

For a buyer, take the parcel address, intended use, and any planned work to the City for zoning and permit-record questions; get the relevant standards before relying on a broad district description. For a seller, assemble available property and improvement records and verify the mapped district before preparing property details. Northglenn’s code says district-boundary changes require an amendment through its rezoning procedures. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts

Questions & answers.

Where can I check a Northglenn property’s zoning district?+

Start with Northglenn’s Official Zoning District Map, which is incorporated into the city code. The code says the map may be examined during office hours at the City Clerk’s office. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Check office hours with the City before visiting, and ask about the exact parcel if the mapped boundary is unclear.

Does a zoning district description confirm what I can build?+

No. The code describes district purpose and intended character, and says district sections also include lot and building standards and district-specific development standards. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Ask the City which standards apply to your parcel and proposed project before treating a general district description as an answer about a specific use or building.

What should I do if a property seems to sit on a zoning boundary?+

Northglenn’s code says boundaries generally follow street, road, highway, alley, or channelized-waterway centerlines unless otherwise stated. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts If a boundary is unclear or disputed, the Director determines its location; an appeal is heard by the Board of Adjustment. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts Ask the City to clarify the parcel.

How do MN, MC, and MR differ?+

MN is intended primarily for residential uses with limited community and neighborhood-serving commercial uses. MC focuses on mixed-use development with active streetscapes, lighter-intensity commercial uses, and attached housing. MR is intended for large-scale development serving the City and region, including regional commercial activity and limited higher-density housing. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts These are purposes, not parcel-specific approvals.

Does the zoning map tell me whether a home has permits?+

The code describes the map as showing zoning-district boundaries; it does not say the map establishes a property’s permit history. Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts As a separate due-diligence step, ask the City about available permit records for the address, and compare those records with the improvements you observe and the information provided by the seller.

Can zoning boundaries change?+

Yes. Northglenn’s code says changes to zoning-district boundaries require an amendment through the rezoning procedures in Subsection 11-6-4(a). Northglenn UDO, Article 11-2: Zoning Districts For a property decision, ask the City whether a boundary question or proposed change affects the parcel; do not assume that a district purpose alone predicts a future amendment or its outcome.
