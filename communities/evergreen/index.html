Find your place in Evergreen.

Explore Evergreen with a realistic look at the home as well as the setting. Begin with the lake area, then investigate access, utilities, insurance and the everyday trips that would shape ownership of the particular property you are considering.

Photo credit: E10ddie

Explore Colorado · A practical guide

Use this guide to compare addresses against your routine, verify routes and parcel-specific requirements, review county application records, and turn unresolved questions into a buying or selling plan.

01

Start with the lake, but check the day’s conditions

Evergreen Lake is a concrete way to get a feel for one part of the community. The lake and its hiking trail are within Dedisse Park, which is owned by the City and County of Denver and managed by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District (EPRD). Evergreen Lake & Trail If you visit, notice how the trail, lakefront and nearby picnic area fit your preferred routine, then compare that experience with the specific locations you are considering.

The lake guide lists seasonal boating and ice skating, trout fishing with a state fishing license, picnic tables and a historical boat and warming house. Evergreen Lake & Trail Before making a visit around boating or skating, call the district’s hotline at 720-880-1391, because the guide says lake and ice conditions change constantly. Evergreen Lake & Trail Treat recreation features as a starting point for comparing addresses, not a substitute for checking access and conditions.

02

Downtown Evergreen has a distinct planning context

Jefferson County describes downtown Evergreen as one of a handful of main street downtowns in unincorporated Jefferson County. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel The county’s account says the area developed before zoning, and that later zoning did not reflect the existing buildings or character. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel For a home search, use this as context for asking about an individual parcel, rather than assuming every property shares the same constraints or potential.

The county’s 2019 Technical Advisory Panel (TAP) examined zoning, transportation, resilience and fiscal questions for downtown. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel Its description identifies parking, connectivity and floodplains as challenges to redevelopment proposals, and says the county wanted to foster downtown’s historic character. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel When comparing a downtown property, ask what rules and site conditions apply to that address and verify answers with the county.

03

Compare addresses against your everyday routine

Use the lake’s documented amenities to make your comparison specific: the guide lists a trail, fishing, picnic tables, seasonal boating and skating, and a historical boat and warming house. Evergreen Lake & Trail Consider which of those features you would actually use, how often you would want to visit, and what you need from the property itself. Those are suggested checks; the source does not establish that any particular home has convenient access.

The picnic tables beside the Evergreen Lake House are first come, first served, and EPRD says it does not take reservations for Dedisse Park. Evergreen Lake & Trail The Evergreen Nature Center has moved from the lake location to 27640 Highway 74, according to the district’s guide. Evergreen Lake & Trail If these destinations matter to your plans, verify their current arrangements and assess the route from each property you are considering.

04

Verify transportation from the actual property

Transportation was one of the subjects assigned to the downtown Evergreen TAP. Its questions included how a revised Highway 74 road template could address vehicle trips, pedestrian walkability and historic character, as well as whether CDOT would continue to allow on-street parking. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel The page presents these as questions considered by the panel, not as confirmed outcomes or current transportation rules.

For a practical comparison, check the routes you would personally use from each address and visit at the times that matter to your plans. This is a suggested check, not a commute estimate. Ask relevant agencies about current road conditions, pedestrian access and parking rules for a specific route or property. The county TAP page records the topics it explored, but does not provide a current route assessment for an individual home. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel

05

Make property questions parcel-specific

Floodplain conditions deserve a direct question when researching downtown property: Jefferson County notes that downtown buildings were built on flatter areas near water, which today translate to floodplains, and describes related county and federal regulations. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel That area-wide explanation does not establish whether a particular parcel is in a floodplain or what rules apply there. Request parcel-specific information and verify it with the appropriate county staff.

Parking and site constraints are also described in the county’s downtown planning background: it says county parking standards favor on-site parking, which can be difficult in an area with small lots and steep topography. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel Treat those points as prompts for investigation, not a verdict on a property. Suggested checks include asking about applicable parking requirements, permitted uses and any known site limitations before relying on a renovation or expansion idea.

06

Check applications, zoning and permit records

Jefferson County directs people seeking information on active cases to its Citizen Portal or Ongoing Applications and Permits Map. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO Use those resources to look for relevant applications, then ask county Planning and Zoning staff how to interpret the records for the address you are considering. The high-interest page is not a parcel-specific zoning determination, so do not infer a property’s rules from a community overview.

The county explains that some application types go to public hearing while others are evaluated administratively; it gives Site Development Plans and Grading Permit Applications as examples of administrative cases. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO For cases that require hearings, the page describes different decision-making boards according to application type. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO Ask which process applies to a specific proposal rather than assuming an application will be approved or follow a particular timeline.

07

Make one visit answer practical questions

Plan a scouting visit around a specific address and a short list of questions. If the lake is part of your routine, walk the trail and check the day’s boating or skating information by calling 720-880-1391. Evergreen Lake & Trail If downtown is relevant, look at the property’s immediate setting and note questions about parking, access or floodplain status for follow-up with the county. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel

Use the visit to test your own priorities rather than to rank parts of Evergreen. Record the route you took, what you want to verify, and any questions about the parcel’s permitted uses or pending applications. Those are suggested steps. If you plan a commercial photo shoot at Evergreen Lake, the district requires a photography permit; its guide says applications are reviewed and communicated within two business days. Evergreen Lake & Trail Confirm current requirements before arranging a shoot.

08

Move from research to a buying or selling plan

For a buyer, turn observations into a property-specific checklist: confirm the applicable zoning and permit history, ask about floodplain or parking questions where relevant, and verify transportation details directly. Jefferson County provides its Citizen Portal and applications map for active-case information, and its Planning and Zoning Division lists 303-271-8700 as a contact number. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO Bring unresolved questions to the appropriate county staff before treating a possibility as a confirmed use.

For a seller, gather the property details and records a prospective buyer may want to investigate, and identify which questions need an official answer. This is a suggested preparation step, not a claim about what any buyer will require. If your property or plans relate to downtown redevelopment, the county TAP page provides planning context but does not decide parcel rules. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel Set next steps around verified facts: request records, confirm current requirements and plan a follow-up visit.

Before your next move

Questions & answers.

Who owns and manages Evergreen Lake?+

Evergreen Lake is part of Dedisse Park, which is owned by the City and County of Denver and managed by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District. Evergreen Lake & Trail For lake questions, the district lists the Lake House at 720-880-1300; for boating and skating conditions, it lists 720-880-1391. Evergreen Lake & Trail

What can visitors do at Evergreen Lake?+

The district lists a hiking trail, fishing for trout with a state fishing license, seasonal boating and ice skating, and picnic tables. Evergreen Lake & Trail The guide says picnic tables beside the Lake House are first come, first served, with no EPRD reservations for Dedisse Park. Evergreen Lake & Trail

Does downtown Evergreen’s planning context determine a parcel’s rules?+

No. Jefferson County describes area-wide downtown issues, including zoning, parking and floodplains, but that overview does not determine the rules for an individual property. Evergreen Technical Advisory Panel Check the specific parcel’s zoning and application records through county resources, and ask Planning and Zoning staff to clarify requirements that affect your plans.

Where can I look for active planning applications?+

Jefferson County directs users to its Citizen Portal or Ongoing Applications and Permits Map for information on active cases. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO Use those tools to research an address, then contact Planning and Zoning if you need help interpreting a record. The county page does not establish parcel rules simply by listing a case.

Are all county planning applications decided at public hearings?+

No. Jefferson County says some cases go to hearing, while others use an administrative process; it names Site Development Plans and Grading Permit Applications as examples of administrative cases. High Interest Cases | Jefferson County, CO The process depends on the application type, so verify the route for the specific case rather than assuming a hearing or decision.

What should I check before planning a lake visit?+

Call the boating and skating hotline at 720-880-1391 before visiting for those activities; the district says lake and ice conditions change constantly. Evergreen Lake & Trail Also note that EPRD does not take reservations for Dedisse Park picnic areas, and tables beside the Lake House are first come, first served. Evergreen Lake & Trail
