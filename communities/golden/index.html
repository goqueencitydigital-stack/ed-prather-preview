Find your place in Golden.

Explore Golden by combining a downtown walk with a realistic transportation test. The goal is to understand how a property supports your ordinary day, not only how appealing the area feels during a weekend visit.

Explore Colorado · A practical guide

Use this guide to plan a focused visit, test the routes and destinations that matter to your household, and make a parcel-specific list of zoning and permit questions to verify with the responsible planning office before making a buying or selling decision.

01

Start with Golden’s downtown and Clear Creek

Golden’s historic downtown sits beside Clear Creek and within a mountain setting that includes North Table, giving a first visit a clear sense of place. Historic Downtown Golden The “Welcome to Golden” Arch, Washington Avenue shops, cafés and boutiques offer useful stops for observing the downtown environment on foot. Historic Downtown Golden Consider whether this mix matches the way you want to spend ordinary days.

For a visit beyond shopping, the city’s official guide points to Parfet Park, the Clear Creek trail, Golden History Museum and Park, and Foothills Art Center. Historic Downtown Golden The guide also describes parks within Golden. Historic Downtown Golden Make a short list of destinations you would actually use, then note how convenient each feels from properties you are considering.

02

Compare locations by your own daily routine

Golden’s downtown experience includes historic architecture, public art, outdoor-gear shops, restaurants and locally made beer. Historic Downtown Golden The official visitor guide identifies the 12th Street Neighborhood as a historic area to explore. Historic Downtown Golden Treat these as destinations to explore, not evidence about the housing, services or character of any particular block.

To compare candidate locations, write down the places and activities that matter to your household, then test the routes yourself at times you would normally travel. This is a suggested check, not a claim that one area is more convenient than another. Golden’s visitor guide identifies downtown shops, parks, trails and museums as possible stops; use the ones relevant to you as comparison points. Historic Downtown Golden

03

Check the actual route, not a commute estimate

Golden’s visitor transportation guide lists I-70, Highway 6, Highway 58, Highway 93 and C-470 as highways connecting to the city. Getting Here & Around It also describes RTD’s W Line between Denver Union Station and Jefferson County Government Center–Golden Station. Getting Here & Around If a route matters to your decision, check its current service and conditions for your own travel times rather than relying on a generalized estimate.

The guide names RTD Route 17, connecting downtown Golden with Oak Station and Federal Center Station, and Route GS, connecting Golden with Boulder and Lakewood. Getting Here & Around It also describes the free Ore Cart community shuttle and its Gold, Silver and Tungsten routes. Getting Here & Around Verify current schedules, stops and service days directly before planning a trip, since routes and operating details may change.

04

Test walking, cycling and parking for yourself

Golden’s visitor guide describes downtown as walkable, with shops, restaurants, attractions and bars close to one another. Getting Here & Around It also points to bike lanes and trails, including routes through downtown and toward the foothills. Getting Here & Around If walking or cycling is part of your routine, try the specific route you would use and check the city’s trail and bike facility map before relying on it.

Golden’s visitor guide describes a mix of street parking and city-operated lots downtown. Getting Here & Around Parking details can change, so check current rates, time limits and posted signs. If a property visit requires parking, test the relevant area at a time that reflects your likely needs.

05

Look up zoning for the specific property

Jefferson County directs users to its Public jMap to find zoning and other property information, including permit activity, tax information and nearby development applications. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? The county says users can search an address, select the property and then consult the corresponding district in the Zoning Resolution. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? Use this parcel-level process rather than inferring rules from a city overview.

The county says district sections of its Zoning Resolution describe allowed uses, setbacks, lot sizes and general building standards. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? For a property inside city limits, the county advises contacting the appropriate municipality about zoning. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? As a suggested due-diligence step, confirm which jurisdiction applies before treating any map result as an answer about a proposed use.

06

Use jMap to guide your follow-up questions

Jefferson County’s jMap is an online mapping resource that can be searched by street address number, parcel identification number, street name or reception number. jMap Online Mapping The county lists zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, recent permit activity, tax information and nearby development applications among the information available through the tool. jMap Online Mapping Treat a map review as an initial research step and record questions that need confirmation.

The county explains that Planned Development, or PD, districts are site-specific and may have different standards and permitted uses; it recommends contacting a planner to determine those details. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? A useful suggested check is to compare the parcel’s map information with the relevant zoning materials and ask the appropriate planning office about unresolved items. Do not assume a nearby parcel has identical rules.

07

Make one scouting visit answer useful questions

Build a visit around a few documented Golden destinations: walk Washington Avenue, see the arch, visit a park or the Clear Creek trail, and choose a museum or art stop that interests you. Historic Downtown Golden Golden’s guide identifies the Golden History Museum and Park, Foothills Art Center and public art walk as cultural options. Historic Downtown Golden Use the outing to assess your own preferences, not to generalize about unvisited areas.

If you are evaluating a specific home, add a separate property-focused checklist: test the routes you expect to use, check parking signs, and note questions about zoning or permit activity. These are suggested checks, not assumptions about the parcel. Jefferson County’s jMap can help locate property information, while the county says PD questions should go to a planner. jMap Online Mapping What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property?

08

Turn your notes into a clear next step

For a purchase, organize your notes into three groups: what you experienced on the visit, what needs parcel-specific verification, and what you still want to learn. Check jMap for available zoning and permit information, then follow up with the relevant planning office about items that remain unclear. jMap Online Mapping The county notes that property inside city limits should be referred to the appropriate municipality for zoning questions. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property?

For a sale, consider preparing a practical property-information file and identifying questions a buyer may want to verify; this is a suggested preparation step, not a claim about required disclosures. If a buyer asks about possible uses or changes, direct the question toward the applicable zoning district and the responsible planning office rather than promising an outcome. The county says zoning rules and standards are found in the district sections of its Zoning Resolution. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property?

Before your next move

Questions & answers.

What can I explore in downtown Golden?+

The official guide highlights the “Welcome to Golden” Arch, Washington Avenue shops and cafés, the historic 12th Street area, Clear Creek trail, parks, museums, Foothills Art Center and public art. Historic Downtown Golden Choose a few stops that match your interests, then explore in person to decide what you would return to.

What public transportation serves Golden?+

The visitor guide lists RTD Route 17, Route GS and the W Line, as well as the free Ore Cart shuttle with Gold, Silver and Tungsten routes. Getting Here & Around Check current RTD and Ore Cart schedules, stops, fares and operating days before relying on a specific connection.

How do I check a Golden property’s zoning?+

For a property under county zoning jurisdiction, Jefferson County directs users to Public jMap to look up the property and consult the matching district in the county Zoning Resolution. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? For property within city limits, the county says to contact the appropriate municipality about zoning. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property?

What information can I find in jMap?+

Jefferson County says jMap can show information such as zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, recent permit activity, tax information and nearby development applications. jMap Online Mapping The tool can be searched by address number, parcel identification number, street name or reception number. jMap Online Mapping Use map findings as a starting point for follow-up questions.

What if a property is zoned Planned Development?+

Jefferson County says Planned Development districts are site-specific, with standards and permitted uses that vary by development. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property? The county recommends contacting a planner to determine the permitted uses and standards for a particular PD district. What is My Zoning? What Can I Do on My Property?
