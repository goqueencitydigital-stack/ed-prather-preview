What areas does Ed Prather Real Estate serve?

The team works from 274 Union Boulevard, Suite 450, in Lakewood and serves Denver, Lakewood and surrounding Front Range communities, including Golden, Arvada, Littleton, Westminster and more.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Request a home valuation. A local agent reviews nearby sales and competing listings, then talks through pricing with you.

Can you help me sell my home and buy my next one?

Yes. One team plans both timelines and works to align your sale and purchase so you know how the two sides of your move fit together.

Do you work with first-time homebuyers?

Yes. The team helps first-time buyers with budget planning, financing preparation and contract basics before and during the search.

Can you help if I am relocating to Denver?

Yes. The team helps out-of-state buyers compare areas and plan a focused search so the move to Colorado feels manageable.

Why do Lakewood clients choose Ed Prather Real Estate?

Ed Prather Real Estate is a Lakewood-based team led by Broker/Owner Ed Prather, a Colorado native with over 20 years in real estate. The team is rated 5.0 across more than 1,000 Google reviews, works from 274 Union Boulevard in Lakewood, and gives each client a dedicated specialist for each stage of the move.

Where can I read reviews of Ed Prather Real Estate?

Client reviews are published on the team’s Google Business Profile, Zillow, Experience.com, FastExpert and Redfin.

How does the team approach work?

Ed Prather Real Estate uses a team-based approach, giving you a dedicated specialist for each stage of your move instead of one agent handling everything alone.
