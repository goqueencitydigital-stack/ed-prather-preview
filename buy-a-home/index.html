GUIDING PRINCIPLES

THE RIGHT HOME. THE RIGHT VALUE.

A beautiful home is exciting, but understanding what you’re buying is just as important. Asking the right questions about price, condition, location, and future expenses can help you make a more informed investment.

At Ed Prather Real Estate, we help you look beyond the listing photos and consider the details that affect a property’s suitability and value.

Our guidance includes:

Comparable home sales

Reviewing recent sales to understand how a property’s asking price compares with relevant local market activity.

Property condition

Considering the age, visible features, and available property information while recognizing when professional inspections are needed.

Ownership expenses

Helping you account for expenses such as property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, and potential maintenance costs.

Market competition

Understanding buyer interest, available inventory, and other factors that may influence your offer.

Long-term suitability

Considering whether a home’s location, layout, and features align with your plans for the future.

Our goal is to help you make decisions based on reliable information, not pressure or guesswork.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT BUYING A HOME

What is the first step to buying a home in Denver?

Start by reviewing your budget, financing options, and preferred locations. Speaking with a real estate professional and getting mortgage pre-approval can help you understand what you can afford and prepare for the home search.

How much money do I need to buy a house in Colorado?

The amount depends on the purchase price, financing program, down payment requirements, closing costs, and other expenses. Some loan programs allow lower down payments than others. A qualified lender can explain available options and help estimate your upfront costs.

Do I need mortgage pre-approval before searching for homes?

You can explore available listings before obtaining pre-approval, but speaking with a lender early is generally helpful. Pre-approval can clarify your purchasing budget and may strengthen your position when you’re ready to submit an offer.

How long does it take to buy a home?

The timeline varies based on your property search, financing, market conditions, and contract terms. Once an offer is accepted, the remaining process typically involves inspections, appraisal, title review, financing, and closing preparations.

Can I buy a home in Denver while living in another state?

Yes. Our team can help out-of-state buyers explore communities, review available properties, coordinate showings where feasible, and understand the steps involved in purchasing a home in Colorado.

What happens after my offer is accepted?

After acceptance, you’ll work through the deadlines and requirements in your purchase agreement. These may involve earnest money, inspections, appraisal, financing, title review, and closing preparations. We’ll help you understand the process and stay informed along the way.
