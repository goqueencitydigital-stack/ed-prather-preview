Website accessibility

Help accessing the website, reporting a barrier, or requesting information another way.

Updated November 1, 2026. We aim to make this website understandable and usable for people with a range of access needs. If you encounter a barrier, contact the team so we can understand the issue and discuss another way to access the information.

Features designed to support access

The website uses headings, labeled form controls, text alternatives for images, visible keyboard focus, and navigation that can be operated with a keyboard. Layouts adapt to smaller screens, and motion is reduced when a reduced-motion preference is set. These features do not amount to a guarantee that every page works with every assistive technology.

Known limitations and alternatives

  • Video: Some video content does not have supplied captions or a text transcript. Contact the team to ask for the information in another format.
  • Wide tables: Some comparisons require horizontal scrolling on small screens. Focusable table regions can be scrolled with the keyboard; contact the team if a table is difficult to use.
  • Third-party services: Property search, maps, social websites, and external content are operated by other providers. If an external service presents a barrier, ask the team for assistance with the underlying real estate question.
  • Interactive tools: Questionnaires and planning tools require JavaScript. You can contact the team by phone or email instead of using an interactive feature.

Testing and conformance

This version has received browser-based checks of responsive layouts, keyboard interactions, navigation, and form controls. A comprehensive independent accessibility audit and broad assistive-technology testing have not been completed. We do not claim certified or complete accessibility conformance.

Report a barrier or request assistance

Contact Ed Prather Real Estate at (720) 650-4959 or team@edprather.com, or write to 274 Union Boulevard, Suite 450, Lakewood, CO 80228. If possible, include the page address, the action you were trying to complete, what went wrong, and the browser or assistive technology involved. You do not need to disclose a disability or provide medical information to report a problem.

Tell the team your preferred way to be contacted and whether another information format would help. Response timing and available alternatives will be discussed with you rather than promised by this statement.
