How It Works

Sell Your House
In 5 Simple Steps

1

Consultation

We assess your property’s market value, using our deep knowledge of the local market and comparable sales. We guide you on setting the right price and developing a custom marketing strategy that will maximize your home’s exposure and appeal to the right buyers.

2

Preparing
Your Home

From staging recommendations to organizing professional photography, we ensure your home looks its best. We handle everything from minor repairs to decluttering, making sure your property shines online and in person. Our attention to detail ensures your home attracts qualified buyers and makes a great first impression.

3

Marketing
& Showings

We leverage a multi-channel marketing strategy, using online listings, social media, and agent networks to get your property in front of the right audience. We organize showings and open houses, ensuring your home is presented to serious buyers. We also provide feedback after each showing, so you stay informed about buyer interest.

4

Offers
& Negotiations

When offers come in, we analyze them with you and provide expert advice on how to respond. We’ll handle negotiations, working to secure the best price and terms that meet your goals. We keep a cool head and a sharp focus on your priorities, helping you avoid common pitfalls and ensure a smooth process.

5

Closing

We guide you through the final steps, ensuring all paperwork is in order and contingencies are addressed. We coordinate with buyers, lenders, and escrow to ensure a seamless closing. After the sale, we’re here for any post-sale questions or future real estate needs.
