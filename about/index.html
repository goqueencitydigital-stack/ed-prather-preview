Put your research to work

What a useful real estate relationship should help you clarify.

The right starting point is an honest conversation about your goals. Explain what is prompting the move, what you need to protect and which decisions you are not ready to make. Ask how communication will work and who will help you keep track of the next step.

Our team directory and individual profiles provide a place to get acquainted before reaching out. Use your initial conversation to discuss the property type, locations and transaction questions relevant to you, rather than assuming every move requires the same approach.

Ask about the working relationship

Clarify the proposed services, how responsibilities will be documented and how compensation is addressed. Colorado’s written brokerage agreement guidance explains the importance of documenting the relationship. Ask your broker to walk through the applicable agreement and seek independent professional advice where needed.

Bring decisions, not just a destination

For a purchase, share your priorities and unresolved budget or property questions. For a sale, bring the address, timing and available property records. If you are coordinating both, explain how access to sale proceeds affects the purchase plan. That information makes a first conversation more specific and useful.
