Put your research to work

How to prepare for a conversation with a Colorado real estate agent.

Use the team profiles to learn about the people behind the website, then make your conversation about your own move. Share the communities you are considering, your timing and whether you are buying, selling or coordinating both. If you are already working with a real estate professional, mention that relationship at the start.

The Colorado Division of Real Estate home-buying guide recommends speaking with more than one broker and choosing someone whose knowledge and approach fit your needs. Ask practical questions about communication, the proposed services and how your responsibilities will be documented.

Questions worth bringing to the introduction

Ask how you will compare properties or evaluate a pricing recommendation, how unanswered condition questions will be handled and what information is needed before the next step. Clarify your preferred communication method and who should be included in important decisions.

Keep your initial message focused

A useful introduction can be short: your intended move, approximate timing, the locations or address involved and the main question you want to resolve. Do not include bank statements, identification documents or account credentials in a general website inquiry.
